**Title:** Exceptional Market Event Router

## Notes / Description

Built an MIT-licensed Exceptional Market Event Router: a reusable GenLayer primitive that classifies documented market disruption without being a price oracle. It freezes a bounded route policy and public HTTPS evidence sources. Leader and validators independently assess the material; custom equivalence compares the consequential route—NORMAL, CLOSE_ONLY, PAUSE_LIQUIDATIONS, FALLBACK_SETTLEMENT, or UNRESOLVED—not output shape. The accepted route selects a precommitted perpetual-market safety action. Private/malformed evidence, source failures, ambiguity, unauthorized calls, and validator disagreement fail closed. Includes pinned GenVM source, direct tests, security audit, test matrix, StudioNet and Bradbury evidence.
